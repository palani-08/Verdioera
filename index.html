The problem at large

The world does not need more “green” labels. It needs better products at scale.

Businesses consume bags, tissues, rolls, food packaging and disposable products every day. Sustainability becomes meaningful when better materials and better manufacturing meet commercial reliability.

Fragmented supply

Multiple suppliers, inconsistent specifications and variable lead times complicate everyday procurement.

Sourcing is not manufacturing

Verdioera's long-term differentiation is capability — not simply placing sourced products into a catalogue.

Scale is the gap

Sustainable alternatives must work at repeat commercial volumes, not only as small trials.

Waste starts upstream

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before you place an order?

A quick guide to manufacturing, specifications, quantities, customisation and B2B supply.

Is Verdioera a manufacturer or a sourcing company?

Verdioera is being built with a manufacturing-first model. Our core manufacturing and conversion capabilities are clearly distinguished from products supplied through qualified partners or categories under development.

Can you manufacture products to our specifications?

Yes. Depending on the product family, specifications can include dimensions, GSM, ply, size, printing, packaging, material and application requirements. Share your requirement through the RFQ form for evaluation.

Do you accept bulk and recurring B2B orders?

Yes. The portfolio is designed for retailers, restaurants, hotels, bakeries, hospitals, textile businesses, institutions, e-commerce companies and other commercial buyers.

Can Verdioera provide private-label or custom-branded products?

Selected product families can be developed with customer-specific branding, printing, sizes and packaging. The feasibility depends on the product, artwork, quantity and production process.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product, specification, material, printing and manufacturing process. The RFQ process allows the team to assess the requirement and provide the applicable MOQ.

Do you manufacture biodegradable products in-house?

Verdioera is developing manufacturing capability across selected biodegradable categories. The website intentionally distinguishes current manufacturing capability from categories under development rather than presenting every product as in-house manufactured.

Can you supply samples before a bulk order?

Sample availability depends on the product and degree of customisation. For custom products, specification and sample approval can form part of the pre-production process.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the RFQ section, select the product family, provide quantity and specifications, and submit your requirement. The quotation can then be prepared based on the actual specification rather than a generic catalogue price.

Do you supply outside Tamil Nadu?

Verdioera is being developed as a B2B supply platform serving customers beyond the immediate manufacturing region. Freight, delivery timelines and commercial terms are evaluated based on destination and order requirements.

Can you help us choose a sustainable alternative?

Yes. Share the product you currently use, application, quantity and required performance. The team can evaluate suitable paper, fibre or biodegradable alternatives and identify the appropriate product family.
